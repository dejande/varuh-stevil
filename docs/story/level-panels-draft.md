Rules the text follows (spec §8):

- One panel per level, 1–2 sentences, set in the chapter's place, readable by a 6-year-old with help.
- `{hero}` only at the start of a sentence or as a direct address, because Slovene names decline. Otherwise the text says "ti".
- Present tense for the hero, so girls and boys get the same sentence and no `{gender, select, …}` is needed.
- Panel 5 of each chapter leads into the boss. What happens after the boss is already frozen in the spec.
- Rogec appears from the start; Ribica, the olm, joins after the cave (chapter 3) and lights the way in chapters 4 and 5.

## Open points for Dejan

- **Boss intros:** the spec has text after each boss but none before it. Panel 5 of each chapter sets the boss up. Should each boss also get its own one-line intro (e.g. »Velikan Kozolec se zbudi in zarohni!«)?
- **Rogec in the lake (2.4):** a small joke that he is afraid of cold water. Keep or drop?
- **The Slovene dual** is used where it fits (»naju«, »vama«, »prideta« for the hero and Rogec). It is correct Slovene; the kids may ask about it, which is not a bad thing.
- **Places** like the bell tower, the island church, the cave guide and the mountain hut are generic Slovene scenes, not real named places. Real names (Bled, Postojna, Pohorje) could be added if wanted, but the spec keeps the story invented.
